### What you'll find here

- **[N] soft play centres** in **[N] towns and cities**, each with its address, a map,
  Google rating, and the facts we have confirmed: café, parking, toddler and baby areas,
  party rooms, SEN sessions, ages and prices where the venue publishes them.
- Pages for each town listing every centre we hold there, and category pages for toddler,
  party and SEN-friendly soft play where a town has enough of them to compare.
- Guides on the blog, written from the same data.

If we don't know something, the page doesn't say it. We'd rather show a short listing than
guess.

### How listings are found and checked

In short: venues come from Google's business listings, we remove anything that isn't
indoor soft play, and we add facts only when a source confirms them. Each fact is stored
with where it came from and when we checked it. Every venue page shows its "Last verified"
date. The full process is on our [methodology page](/methodology).

### Venue owners

Owners can [claim their listing](/add-venue) free and update their own details. We check
that a claim comes from the venue (usually by its website's email domain). Owners can
also pay for a Premium or Bespoke plan. That buys featured placement: the venue is shown
first in its town's list and always labelled "Featured" or "Sponsored". Paying never changes
what a listing says or its rating.
