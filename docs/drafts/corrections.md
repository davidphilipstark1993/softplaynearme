## Corrections

We want every listing to be right. If something is wrong, out of date or missing, please
tell us.

**How to report:** use "Report a mistake" on the venue's page, or email
info@softplaynearme.co.uk with the venue's name and what has changed. If you can, include a
link to where it says so (the venue's website or social media).

**What happens next:**

- We reply within **[two working days]**.
- We check the change against the venue's own website or with the venue. When we can
  confirm it, we update the listing and its "Last verified" date, usually within
  **[five working days]**.
- A venue that has **closed for good** is removed. Its page explains that the listing has
  been removed.
- If we can't confirm a change, we leave the fact out rather than show something we're
  unsure of.

**Venue owners** can [claim their listing](/add-venue) and make changes themselves.

**Guides and articles:** when we correct a factual error in a blog post, we fix it and
update the post's "last updated" date. **[Optional: add a one-line note at the end of the
post saying what changed.]**

**Something we got badly wrong?** Email the same address with "Complaint" in the subject
and **[your name]** will reply personally.
