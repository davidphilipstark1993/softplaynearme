## 2. Confirmed issues

**High**
1. **740 non-core venues are indexable and in the sitemap** (336 park playgrounds, 147 attractions, 99 leisure centres, 71 mobile hire, 42 farms, 40 trampoline parks, plus a few others). `lib/venueTypes.ts` deliberately treats park-playground / attraction / farm / trampoline as *not* soft play, yet each gets a full indexable page. Example: `/soft-play/hull/abbot-s-walk-children-s-play-area-hull` is a `Park` in its JSON-LD but its live `<title>` says "Children's Soft Play Centre, Hull". Risks thin/misleading content and dilutes the "soft play" topical focus. *Decision needed: noindex and drop from sitemap, or keep with accurate titles.*
2. **Image coverage is 19%** (283 of 1,829). Most cards will show fallbacks. Biggest visible-quality gap, and needs licensed or owner-supplied images, not scraping.
3. **Price data is 8%**; `priceFrom` populated for 9 venues. Cannot be invented. Parents' most-asked question is unanswered for 92% of venues.
4. **`aggregateRating` JSON-LD is emitted from imported Google ratings** (`lib/schema.ts:207`). Google's review-snippet policy requires ratings to be collected by the site itself; imported third-party ratings risk a manual action and conflict with the brief's "no fabricated ratings". Recommend removing it from the markup and showing the rating visibly with source attribution.
5. **Claims endpoint has no abuse protection** (`app/api/claims/route.ts`): no rate limit, no honeypot, and it runs `CREATE TABLE` / `ALTER TABLE` DDL on every request. Verification is email-only, with no check that the claimant's email domain matches the venue's website, so fraudulent claims are possible.
6. **Newsletter is single opt-in**: `subscribe` inserts straight to `status='subscribed'`, with no confirmation email. For UK PECR/GDPR best practice, and to protect SendGrid reputation against typo and malicious sign-ups, use double opt-in.

**Medium**
7. All rate limiters (report, newsletter) are in-memory per serverless instance, so effectively decorative on Vercel. They need a shared store (Upstash/Vercel KV or a DB table).
8. 32 groups of venues share a normalised address (possible duplicates). 6 likely-duplicate groups are already parked in `reports/phase4-geo-followup.md`.
9. City page vs blog "best soft play in X" cannibalisation (carried over from `AUDIT.md`). 18 blog city guides still name zero real venues.
10. ~51% of venues added since July lack phone/website/hours. This is an enrichment gap, not a code bug.
11. Live city pages take 1.0–1.5 s TTFB (`/soft-play/hull` 1.5 s, `/soft-play/london` 1.0 s) against 0.2–0.5 s elsewhere. Worth profiling.
